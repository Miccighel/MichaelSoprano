subtitle: Last updated on August 1, 2026
---

This website is static and does not run a server-side application. It stores the preference for viewing the site in dark mode locally in your browser.

The site loads fonts, libraries, and the Netlify Identity widget from external providers. It also uses Mapbox to display the map in the contact section. These providers may process technical information such as your IP address and browser data under their own privacy policies.

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you enter are submitted to Formspree so that I can receive and reply to your request. You may also choose to contact me through email or the social networks linked on this website.
