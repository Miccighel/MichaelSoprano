About

Welcome to my personal webpage.
I am a Researcher at the University of Udine, affiliated with the Social, Mobile, Data & Crowd Laboratory.
I hold a PhD in Computer Science and Mathematical and Physical Sciences.

My research interests center on Human Computation, Crowdsourcing, and Information Retrieval.
I am currently focusing on the use of crowdsourcing to address the growing challenge of misinformation.

You can download my Curriculum Vitae in English or Italian.
Feel free to also explore my PhD thesis, together with my Master's, Bachelor's, and High School theses (in Italian).
