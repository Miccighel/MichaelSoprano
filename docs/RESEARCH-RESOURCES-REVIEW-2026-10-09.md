# Research resources review — 2026-10-09

Scope: all 38 publication records. Extracted full PDF text and embedded URI annotations; only resources explicitly associated with the publication were added. References to third-party datasets, generic libraries and unrelated contributions in proceedings were excluded. AIUCD was limited to the author's contribution (PDF pages 253–256).

Reviewed 36 full texts (27 retrieved copies plus nine distinct author PDFs supplied by the user). Retrieval failures do not imply that no resources exist. NewBestSub is explicitly released in the JDIQ manuscript; its association with SIGIR 2018 is grounded in the repository README rather than an explicit release URL in that paper. Existing Crowd_Frame and TSC links were retained and confirmed against their supplied PDFs.

Labels: **Dataset** for data-focused releases (including tabular results, corpora and documentation); **Code** for implementation-focused repositories; **Materials** for mixed study archives containing data alongside executable analysis, task configurations, survey instruments or other supplementary resources. Classification follows the inspected destination contents, not its hosting platform or the paper's release wording. No duplicate buttons for the same archive. No bibliographic metadata, citation metrics or CV files were changed in this pass.
