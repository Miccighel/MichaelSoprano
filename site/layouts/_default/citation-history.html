How to read the chart

Filled dots add up the citations received through each year. An open circle marks a separately checked Scholar total when it differs from the sum or was checked in a later year. Select a point to see its count and verification date.

With the chart focused, use Left and Right to move through years and Up and Down to switch publications. Home and End jump to the first and last points. Enter highlights the publication; Escape closes the tooltip.
