title: Privacy Policy
subtitle: Last updated on August 1, 2026

This website is static and does not run a server-side application. It stores the preference for viewing the site in dark mode and the cookie notice locally in your browser.

The site loads fonts and interface libraries from external providers. The contact map uses OpenStreetMap tiles. These providers may process technical information such as your IP address and browser data under their own privacy policies.

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you enter are submitted to Formspree so that I can receive and reply to your request. You may also choose to contact me through email or the social networks linked on this website.
