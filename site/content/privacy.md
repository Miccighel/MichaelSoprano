---
title: Privacy Policy
subtitle: Last updated on September 1, 2026
---

This website is static and does not run a server-side application. It stores only the light or dark appearance preference in your browser's local storage. The site itself does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Fonts and interface libraries are served directly by this website. The contact map requests map tiles from OpenStreetMap when the homepage is loaded; OpenStreetMap may process technical information such as your IP address and browser data under its own privacy policy.

If you submit the contact form, the name, email address, and message you enter are sent to Formspree so that I can receive and reply to your request. No form data is sent until you choose to submit it. You may instead contact me directly by email or through one of the profiles linked on this website.
