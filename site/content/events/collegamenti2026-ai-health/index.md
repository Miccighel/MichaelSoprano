A public outreach workshop curated by Eddy Maddalena and Michael Soprano on October 4, 2026, as part of Festival Collega-Menti at the University of Udine.

The workshop explores the use of artificial intelligence in healthcare and how contributions from people without specialist medical training could support the collection of data for training AI systems, including the analysis of histological images.

Booking is required through the [official programme](https://festivalcollegamenti.it/collegamenti2026/programma/45/intelligenza-artificiale-e-salute/).
