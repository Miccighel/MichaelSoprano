## About this edition

- **Degree:** Bachelor's Degree in Sports Science, University of Udine
- **Course:** *Sistemi di Analisi ed Elaborazione delle Informazioni per l'Allenamento*
- **Instructor:** Michael Soprano, course instructor
- **Teaching hours:** 24
- **Assessment:** Written exam with answer choices

## Programme

The final programme presented in the closing slides of this edition covered three modules.

### 1. Data science in sport

- What data are and how they are used in sport
- From raw data to information
- Populations, variables, measurement scales and types of data

### 2. Representation and management of data

- Filesystem storage and ways of representing data
- An introduction to semiotics
- Digital representation of text, images, sound and video, including character encoding, graphics, sampling and MIDI messages
- The Shannon–Weaver model, entropy and redundancy
- Lossless and lossy compression

### 3. Data analysis with Microsoft Excel

- Workbook interface, cells, data entry, formatting and cell ranges
- A practical case study on a gym budget
- Formulas and functions, including syntax, operators, arguments and common errors
- Importing structured data from text files
- Creating charts, including line, scatter and area charts
- Creating and using PivotTables
