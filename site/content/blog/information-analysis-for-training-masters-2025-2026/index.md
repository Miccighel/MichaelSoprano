## About this edition

- **Degree:** Master's Degree in Sciences and Techniques of Preventive and Adapted Physical Activities, University of Udine
- **Course:** *Sistemi di Analisi ed Elaborazione delle Informazioni per l'Allenamento*
- **Instructor:** Michael Soprano, course instructor
- **Teaching hours:** 24
- **Assessment:** Written exam with questions requiring written answers

## Programme

The teaching materials begin with a review of data, information, variables and measurement in sport. The course itself then develops two modules, as confirmed by its concluding slides.

### 1. Introduction to data mining

- The workflow of a data science project, from preparation and exploration to modelling and interpretation
- Typical tasks such as classification, regression and clustering
- Supervised and unsupervised learning
- Training and test data, validation and measures for evaluating models
- Data quality and methodological issues, including missing values, outliers and temporal consistency

### 2. Relational databases

- Database systems and the conceptual, logical and physical stages of design
- Modelling entities, attributes, relationships, cardinalities and hierarchies
- Translating conceptual models into relational schemas with keys and integrity constraints
- Redundancy, update anomalies and normalization through first, second and third normal form
- A practical case study on cycling races, from requirements to a relational database
- Creating and exploring a SQLite database with DBeaver
- SQL for defining tables and constraints, then inserting, updating and deleting data
- Queries with selection, filtering, ordering, aggregation, grouping and joins
- Exporting results to CSV or Excel for further analysis and reporting
