## Programme

The programme for 2026/2027 is being finalised. The course usually explores two questions: how can we study the connections between people on social media, and how can we design tasks that let people contribute their judgements?

For an example of the practical work, see the [2025/2026 edition](/post/social-computing/2025-2026/), where students designed a task to collect judgements about political statements.
