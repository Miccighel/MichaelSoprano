## Programme

### Module 1: Data science in sport

- **Sources and measurement scales.** Heart rate readings, GPS tracks and athletes’ own reports illustrate different types of sport data. Qualitative and quantitative variables use different scales: nominal, ordinal, interval or ratio. A finishing position gives an order; race times also tell us the size of the gap.
- **Interpreting results.** Organising observations in tables and choosing a chart help reveal patterns. How far a result applies beyond the athletes measured depends on how well the sample represents the population.

### Module 2: Representation and management of multimedia data

- **Files and text.** Files, folders, paths and metadata describe how data are organised and located. Character sets and encodings specify how text is stored in bytes.
- **Images.** Raster images use pixels; vector graphics describe shapes.
- **Sound and video.** Sampling rate and bit depth determine how a device records sound. Resolution and frame rate affect the detail captured in a sequence of images.
- **Communication and compression.** The Shannon and Weaver model describes how a message travels through a channel. Entropy and redundancy explain why some data are easier to compress. Lossless methods preserve the original data; lossy methods discard detail to reduce file size.

### Module 3: Data analysis with Microsoft Excel

- **Preparing data.** Power Query imports records from text files. Worksheets, ranges and formats organise numbers, dates and times for analysis.
- **Formulas and functions.** Cell references, operators and nested functions support calculations such as converting race times to minutes and assigning runners to age groups.
- **Charts.** Fitbit activity records illustrate how to choose between line and area charts for trends over time and scatter plots for relationships between steps, calories and active minutes.
- **PivotTables.** Grouping results from the 2025 Boston Marathon by age group or gender makes it possible to compare finish times using counts, averages and percentages.
- **Dashboards.** A marathon dashboard brings key figures and performance categories together. A PivotChart displays the results for the groups selected in the PivotTable.
