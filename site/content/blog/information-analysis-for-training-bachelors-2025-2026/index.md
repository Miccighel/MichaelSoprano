## About this edition

- **Degree:** Bachelor's Degree in Sports Science, University of Udine
- **Course:** *Sistemi di Analisi ed Elaborazione delle Informazioni per l'Allenamento*
- **Instructor:** Michael Soprano, course instructor
- **Teaching hours:** 24
- **Assessment:** Written exam with answer choices

## Programme

The final programme presented in the closing slides of this edition covered three modules.

### 1. Data science in sport

- Data and information in sport, and the transition from raw data to information
- Populations, variables and measurement scales
- Types of variables and data

### 2. Representation and management of multimedia data

- Filesystem storage and digital representation of data
- Text: character sets and encoding
- Images: raster and vector graphics, and principal formats
- Sound: sampling, sampling frequency and depth
- Video: image sequences, characteristics and formats
- The Shannon–Weaver communication model, entropy and redundancy
- Lossless and lossy compression

### 3. Data analysis with Microsoft Excel

- Workbooks, cells, rows, columns, ranges, data entry and formatting
- Formulas and functions, including references, operators, errors, result types and nesting
- Importing structured data from text files
- Creating line, scatter and area charts
- Creating and using PivotTables
- Practical work with Fitbit data and data from the 2025 Boston Marathon
