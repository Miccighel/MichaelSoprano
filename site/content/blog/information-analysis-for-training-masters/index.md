## About the course

This module develops a structured approach to data used in training and physical activity. It belongs to the Master's Degree in Sciences and Techniques of Preventive and Adapted Physical Activities at the University of Udine.

## Teaching

I am the course instructor. The editions listed above give the teaching hours and programme for each year.

## Topics

- Data quality, requirements and conceptual models
- Relational database design and normalization
- Building and querying sport and training datasets with SQLite, DBeaver and SQL
