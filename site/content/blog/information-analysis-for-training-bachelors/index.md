## About the course

This module introduces students to digital information and practical data analysis in sport and training. It belongs to the integrated course *Bioengineering and Biomechanics of Physical and Sport Activities* in the Bachelor's Degree in Sports Science at the University of Udine.

## Teaching

I am the course instructor. The editions listed above give the teaching hours, programme and assessment for each year.

## Topics

- Foundations of data science and data used in sport and training
- Digital representation, storage and management of information
- Data preparation, analysis and visualization with Microsoft Excel
