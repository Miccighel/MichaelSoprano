## About this edition

Michael Soprano was the course instructor for the Master's Degree in Management Engineering at the University of Udine. The course comprised 24 lectures (48 hours); assessment was based on a full-stack project and discussion.

## Programme

The programme presented at the beginning of this edition included:

1. **The Web and HTTP:** browsers, servers and standards; resources and their identifiers; client-server communication, HTTP methods, status codes and headers; practical work with cURL and Postman.
2. **HTML and CSS:** document structure, forms and media; selectors, the cascade, the box model and modern layout techniques.
3. **JavaScript:** programming basics, the DOM, events and asynchronous requests.
4. **Backend development:** Node.js and Express.js routing and middleware; RESTful APIs and data persistence with SQLite.

The final lecture brought the frontend, backend and storage themes together in a full-stack application. The [course overview](/post/web-applications/) provides the broader context. The lecture slides for this year are not published on the site.
