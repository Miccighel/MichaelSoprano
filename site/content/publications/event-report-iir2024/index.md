abstract: IIR 2024, the 14th Italian Information Retrieval Workshop, served as the
  annual event for the IR and RS communities both in Italy and collaborating with
  Italian research institutions. This year's event spanned two days and featured studies
  on various topics within IR, RS, and Large Language Models (LLMs). Key focus areas
  included enhanced retrieval models, personalized information systems, conversational
  interfaces and user-centric systems, comparative evaluations and metrics, and practical
  applications in specific fields. IIR 2024 was jointly organized by the University
  of Udine and the University of Milano-Bicocca and was held in Udine, Italy.
